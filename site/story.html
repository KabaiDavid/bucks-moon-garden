Our Story · Est. 1992

Make every momentspecial.

1992

An experience guests would never forget.

Established in 1992, Buck wanted to create an experience for guests that they would never forget.

So he created his very own “moon garden,” filling the restaurant and bar with white flowers against dark green walls and twinkling candles. Buck then crafted a fine continental menu, selected artfully mismatched china, and provided nightly live piano music to create the perfect fine dining experience. Thus, Buck’s Restaurant was born.

  2. ii.A fine continental menuToday led by Chef Matthew Klein, with Kentucky Proud ingredients.
  3. iii.Artfully mismatched chinaNo two place settings quite alike.
  4. iv.Live pianoMusic as part of the evening, as Buck intended.
